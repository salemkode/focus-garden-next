## Major

4. **The premise overstates what the contract proves and what withering costs.** Slides 2–3 and the public drafts say the tree is locked for “exactly” the session or that its existence attests to focus. Slide 3's live speaker notes say “public, unforgeable proof.” The covenant enforces sequence age in 512-second units. Maturity is measured from the median time past of the block before confirmation. The app handles tab-away withering by marking release consent forfeited. The covenant still permits a mature claim signed by the owner or the planting fee wallet, and the app has a manual Send action for mature trees. The leaderboard awards points for confirmed plantings even if the timer withers or the NFT is never released. **Fix.** Across the pitch, story, guide and app-facing copy, say “The app tracks whether you stay. Bitcoin Cash records your time-locked pledge. Completing the timer keeps your automatic wallet delivery.” Describe the leaderboard as pledged focus time. Show maturity as a separate milestone and remove “unforgeable proof,” “exactly,” and language implying on-chain forfeiture. Evidence: slides 2–7 and live notes 3–4 and 7, `SUBMISSION.md:13–18,30–38,48–50`, `BUIDL-DRAFT.md:39–49`, `FocusForest.cash:130–161`, `packages/protocol/src/garden.ts:71–88`, `packages/protocol/src/leaderboard.ts:91–101,132–145`, `packages/mobile/src/features/rewards/auto-release.ts:65–83`, `packages/mobile/src/components/RewardActions.tsx:65–69,92–101`.
5. **The release promise is broader than the tested state machine.** The screen starts the timer before submitting and shows a locally grown tree even if the chain write fails. Auto-release matches a consent by time, duration and recipient against claimable chain outputs. It consumes consent before the attempt, including failures before a claim can be broadcast. The existing unit tests exercise matching and forfeiture in isolation. A successful claim therefore requires more evidence than a mature-looking tree or a promise to reopen the tab later. **Fix.** In the proof run capture the successful lock indicator, test close/reopen after the local timer has completed, and document the manual “Verified trees → Ready to send” path for a mature reward that needs it. If hardening release, tie consent to the actual planted commitment and a completed local session, and preserve an actionable state when a claim fails. Evidence: `packages/mobile/src/app/(tabs)/index.tsx:95–117,176–194`, `packages/mobile/src/features/rewards/RewardProvider.tsx:206–229`, `packages/mobile/src/features/rewards/auto-release.ts:40–55`, `packages/mobile/tests/auto-release.test.ts:18–65`.
6. **The guide's “five minutes” path obscures the first useful result.** The owner spends the opening 55 seconds navigating or funding, then the script opens an explorer tab during a strict session. Following that literally can wither the live example. Joining alone also does not “put you on the leaderboard” as promised. The reader filters out profiles with zero confirmed planting points. The fee picker requires one plain BCH output of at least 8,000 sats for Plant, regardless of aggregate balance, and a later Claim needs its own eligible coin. **Fix.** Open the video on a grown valley and the already confirmed lock receipt. Show linked wallet → one 15,000-sat transfer → shortest verified Plant → where to see the lock transaction, in that order. Keep the app foregrounded and show the explorer on a second device or in an edited cut. Label the separate wither clip Simulated or practice, with time-compressed claim playback explicitly marked. State that the initial path ends at broadcast and that wallet arrival comes later. Put optional Join after Plant and say when ranking begins. Evidence: `DEMO.md:20–59`, `packages/landing/index.html:71–83`, `packages/protocol/src/build.ts:16–21,80–91,147–158`, `packages/protocol/src/leaderboard.ts:125–147`.
7. **The valley ladder and the CashTokens tiers disagree in both naming and behavior.** Slide 4 visibly shows Seedling, Sapling and Grand tree. Slides 3 and 5 teach Sprout, Tree, Big Tree and House. A 43-minute UI Grand tree is five units, while the verified reward is tier 2, “Tree,” until six units. The valley uses three Saplings to reach Grand tree and continues through ten levels. The covenant uses two tier-2 Trees for Big Tree and ends at House. The Focus timer tops out at seven units (59m44s), making “over an hour” wrong for that entry point. Trees already delivered to the linked wallet cannot be merged by the covenant. **Fix.** Use the app's actual names for the valley. Label the four on-chain tiers separately, remove the misleading shared recipe, and avoid demoing a five-unit conversion as an equivalent NFT. Show a local merge as local or use matured in-contract inputs for a confirmed covenant merge. Evidence: slides 1, 3–6 and 9, `SUBMISSION.md:30–36`, `packages/garden/src/ladder.ts:24–36`, `packages/mobile/src/features/focus/catalog.ts:61–66,78–93`, `packages/mobile/src/app/(tabs)/index.tsx:108–115`, `packages/protocol/src/forest.ts:12–17`, `packages/protocol/src/build.ts:159–172`, `packages/mobile/src/components/RewardActions.tsx:65–69`.

Say “metadata published” until a named wallet/build displays a freshly claimed NFT. Use Tested locally for the builder, policy, signer/VM and applicable contract families. Publish one fresh suite breakdown and link the narrower checks. Use Pending for unreproduced native blocking and real Selene/Paytaca pairing where appropriate. ## Verdict and track recommendation

**Mainnet only, conditional on the public write path, complete signed-out banner, correct DoraHacks track and Telegram status before 22 November at 14:00 UTC.** The project has independently corroborated Mainnet deployment, and the shortest competitive demonstration is a single verified seedling pledge with a linked lock receipt and a later wallet delivery. Community Choice follows with a valid owner-controlled voting address. Media is allowed alongside Mainnet, but the current submission points at a stale interactive preview and a second entry would need a distinct, current media piece. Skip both XO bounties. The stock native XO P2PKH template use does not provide the required novel or migrated project template, reproducible invitation and debug-tool transaction, and both bounty pages require author-written feedback.
